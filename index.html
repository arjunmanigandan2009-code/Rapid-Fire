How to Play Rapid Fire

Rules & tips for students

🔢 Mode 1: Rapid Flash Numbers

Numbers flash one after another. You keep the running sum in your head!

  • Level 1: Only positive numbers (+ve) - great for building addition speed.
  • Level 2: Positive and negative numbers (+ve & -ve) - practise swift mental subtraction.
  • Level 3: Includes (×) and (÷) operations in between - ultimate mental math workout!
  • Time Delay: Adjust how fast the numbers flash, from 0.5 sec (speed demon) to 3.0 sec (relaxed pace).
  • At the end, choose the correct answer from the 4 options!
⚡ Mode 2: Two Numbers Speed Reflex

An equation with two numbers is presented. Your mission is speed!

  • The Faster You Respond, The More Points You Earn:
    Answers in under 1.0s score up to 1000 points. Points drop as the reaction timer ticks.
  • Combos: Answering multiple questions in a row triggers Fire Streaks and multipliers!
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
  • Press [1], [2], [3], [4] on your keyboard to instantly select answers.
  • Press [Space] or [Enter] to proceed to the next question.
  • Press [Esc] to exit to the hub.
